Coming to the App Store

Soothsay, a fortune teller

A 616 Labs app

Ask a question, typed or spoken. The glass gathers, and a reader in a candlelit room answers in two or three dry sentences.

For iPhone and iPad. In beta on TestFlight. App Store name: Soothsay — Ask the Glass.

What Soothsay does

  • Most answers come from a hand-written set; some are composed on the device by Apple Intelligence.
  • Ask by typing or by speaking.
  • Save a reading to keep it, or let it go.
  • Soothsay is entertainment. It gives no medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

Soothsay support

Write to brian@616labs.net with questions or problems. Please say which device you use and which version of Soothsay.

You can also send a reading you think went wrong from inside the app, with "Report a reading" in Settings.

If you are struggling, help is one tap away in the app's Settings. In the US you can call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, at any time.

Soothsay privacy

Soothsay has no account, no analytics and no server. Your questions and readings stay on your device. Read the Soothsay privacy policy.
