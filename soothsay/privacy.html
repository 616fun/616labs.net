Soothsay privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Summary

Soothsay does not collect, transmit or store your personal information. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics, and 616 Labs runs no server that receives anything from the app.

What stays on your device

  • Your questions. A question exists only for as long as it takes to answer it, unless you choose to save a reading.
  • Saved readings. If you save one, the question and its answer are stored on your device and nowhere else. Unpinned readings are deleted automatically after the retention period you choose in Settings (90 days by default). You can delete any reading at any time, and "Start over" in Settings erases all of them.
  • Your settings. Preferences such as the personality slider are stored on your device.

Deleting the app removes all of it.

How answers are produced

Most answers come from a fixed list written into the app. Some readings are generated on your device using Apple's Foundation Models framework (Apple Intelligence). That happens on the device: your question is not sent to us or to any third party. On devices without Apple Intelligence, the app uses the written responses instead and works the same way.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose to speak a question, the app uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition to turn your speech into text. The audio is used only to transcribe that question. It is not recorded, stored or sent to us.

Where your device supports on-device speech recognition, transcription happens on the device. If it does not, Apple's speech recognition may process the audio on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy. Speaking is optional: the app works fully by typing, and you can decline or revoke microphone access at any time in iOS Settings.

Location and weather

Soothsay can answer questions about the weather. To do that, and only that, it needs to know roughly where you are.

The first time you ask about the weather, iOS asks whether the app may use your location. Nothing is requested before then, and nothing breaks if you decline: the app says it cannot see the sky, and everything else works as before.

If you allow it, your approximate location is used to fetch the current conditions from Apple's WeatherKit under Apple's privacy policy. Your location is used for the weather and nothing else. It is not stored by the app, not saved with your readings, not attached to your questions and never sent to us. You can revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

Soothsay is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

What Soothsay is not

Soothsay is entertainment. It does not provide medical, legal, financial or psychological advice, and its answers should not be relied on as such.

The app is built to recognise questions about self-harm or someone being in danger, set the fortune-telling aside and point to real help. It is tested against hundreds of ways people phrase these questions, but no program can recognise every one, and some will still receive an ordinary answer. If you are struggling, please don't wait for the app to notice: help is one tap away in the app's Settings. In the US you can call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, at any time; outside the US, Settings links to a directory of crisis lines in your country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
