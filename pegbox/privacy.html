Pegbox privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Summary

Pegbox does not collect, transmit or store your personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising, and 616 Labs runs no server that receives anything from the app.

Microphone

Pegbox uses the microphone to measure the pitch of your instrument. The sound is analysed on your device, as you play. It is not sent to 616 Labs or anyone else.

If you record a take for intonation practice, the recording is saved on your device only. It leaves the device only if you choose to share or export it yourself.

Settings and set-ups

Your instruments, tunings and preferences are stored on your device. If you export a backup, the file goes wherever you choose to save or send it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Purchases

If Pegbox offers in-app purchases, Apple handles the payment. 616 Labs does not receive your payment details.

Children

Pegbox collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.

Contact

Questions about privacy: brian@616labs.net. Pegbox is made by 616 Labs, Indianapolis, Indiana.
